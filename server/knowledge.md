## Licensing course: ISSA Inshore Skipper

7-day certification course · day & night sailing · coastal waters · 2 international licences.

Goal: the skills and confidence to skipper a sailing yacht up to 24 m in coastal waters, within 20 NM of the
coastline, in moderate weather. Theory and practical seamanship. After passing you get the ISSA Inshore Skipper
licence and can progress to ISSA Offshore Skipper, the next level.

### Requirements
- Minimum age 16.
- At least 1 sailing cruise done before.
- VHF / SRC radio operator certificate (it is included in both packages).
- Enrolment only through an ISSA-accredited school; the instructor is Inshore Instructor level or higher.

### Where and how
- Location: Greece, Keramoti or Athens (depending on club availability). Sailing in the Aegean Sea.
- Boat: private charter monohull sailing yacht.
- Price includes: ISSA certification fee, instruction, boat and diesel.
- Language: Romanian with English nautical terms; books and exams in English. English or Spanish on request.

### Day by day
1. Orientation & yacht familiarisation: safety briefing, yacht systems, engine checks, knots & line handling.
2. Sailing fundamentals & crew management: sail handling, sheets & halyards, crew roles, handling under sail.
3. Navigation theory & chart work: terrestrial navigation, chart reading, compass, position fixing.
4. Coastal pilotage & electronic navigation: practical pilotage, GPS, AIS, navigational aids & buoyage.
5. Passage planning, meteorology & night sailing: weather, logbook, routing, night navigation & lights.
6. MOB, anchoring & emergencies: man-overboard drills under sail and power, anchoring with crew, emergencies.
7. Assessment & review: practical assessment, theory review, debrief, certification paperwork.

### Curriculum
- Theory: yacht construction & systems; anchoring; inboard & outboard engines; safety procedures; lines, springs &
  fenders; crew management; handling under power; man overboard; pilotage & coastal navigation; collision
  regulations (COLREGs); navigational aids & buoyage; terrestrial & electronic navigation; restricted visibility;
  night navigation & lights; passage planning; logbook keeping; international signalling code; sail handling;
  dinghy operation; meteorology; ecology & social skills.
- Practical: safe onboard operations; yacht systems; water & fuel; inboard and outboard engine operation & faults;
  knots & lines; fenders; anchoring with crew; emergencies; crew management; handling under sail and power; MOB
  approach and recovery; pilotage; night sailing; chart plotting; magnetic compass; GPS; passage planning;
  bearing lines & fixes; dinghy; practical meteorology.
